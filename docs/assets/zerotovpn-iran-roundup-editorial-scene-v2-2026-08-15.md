## Origin and visual direction

These are original ZeroToVPN editorial assets generated with the built-in image generation tool. The hero and landscape source frames were generated separately so the website does not enlarge or heavily crop one low-resolution image across every placement.

The direction is deliberately photographic and restrained: an everyday Tehran balcony, the city, the Alborz mountains, the Milad Tower, tea and a phone in natural late-afternoon light. It avoids the former map collage, neon network paths and futuristic cyber styling.

## Evidence boundary

The scene is an editorial reconstruction. It is not a photograph taken by ZeroToVPN in Iran, a live network-status image, a provider screenshot, a connection test or proof that a VPN currently works on an Iranian network. Keep that boundary visible in the adjacent caption and alternative text.

## Generation prompts

### Hero source

Photorealistic documentary/editorial view from an ordinary Tehran rooftop or apartment balcony in late-afternoon daylight, with authentic city buildings, the Alborz mountains and Milad Tower. A wooden table with tea and an everyday phone anchors the foreground. Natural imperfections, realistic haze and subtle film grain. No map silhouette, flags, provider branding, text, cyber graphics, neon, holograms or fabricated app interface.

### Landscape source

A second wide frame of the same believable Tehran balcony scene, composed as a real 16:9 photograph rather than stretching the portrait. Milad Tower sits near the right third, with the mountains and city across the frame and the foreground objects kept secondary. The same natural-light and no-cyber constraints apply.

## Usage rules
