# Editorial brief: VPN obfuscation and stealth modes

## Why this page exists

The DataForSEO protocol dossier returned recurring PAA questions around “what is VPN obfuscation?”, whether an obfuscated VPN is safe, whether an employer can identify it and which providers document the feature. Existing country pages mention obfuscation but do not provide one canonical technical explanation.

## Intent and scope

Target informational readers who may later compare providers for a restrictive network. Explain the boundary between traffic presentation and anonymity, compare protocol/platform requirements and provide a lawful, reproducible test plan. Do not promise that any provider bypasses a named firewall or works permanently.

## Internal-link role

- Parent commercial pillar: `/best/best-vpn`
- Technical sibling: `/guides/vpn-protocols-explained`
- Country/use-case siblings: Iran, Russia, China and Telegram pages
- Commercial conversion: contextual shortlist only, with the shared disclosure and `sponsored nofollow` link contract

## Evidence policy

- Use official provider documentation for feature existence and requirements.
- Use dated government/regulator or employer guidance for legality and policy questions.
- Label every local result with device, OS, app version, network, protocol, server region and date.
- Treat DataForSEO volume/PAA as prioritisation signals, never as proof of safety or connectivity.
