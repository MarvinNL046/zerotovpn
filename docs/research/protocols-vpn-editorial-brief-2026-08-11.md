# Editorial brief: VPN protocols, WireGuard vs OpenVPN, and obfuscation

## Search signal

DataForSEO US/English research fetched August 11, 2026 shows the strongest explicit demand around **wireguard vs openvpn** (current volume 1,600, KD 8) and **openvpn tcp vs udp** (170, KD 0). The broad guide term is smaller, while PAA questions repeatedly ask which protocol to choose, which is hardest to block, whether WireGuard is safe, and when TCP is preferable.

## Search intent and angle

Build a decision page, not a protocol glossary. Start with a one-screen verdict, then let readers choose by use case: everyday speed, unstable/mobile networks, restrictive networks, and streaming/gaming. Explain that a protocol feature is not proof of availability or legality on a particular network.

## Page structure

1. Hero with updated date, editorial disclosure and a short “choose this when” verdict.
2. Quick decision cards with contextual, sponsored provider links.
3. WireGuard vs OpenVPN table: transport, trade-offs, privacy caveats, and what to test.
4. OpenVPN TCP vs UDP block with the speed/reliability trade-off.
5. Obfuscation/stealth explanation that avoids “undetectable” claims and links to the China, Russia, Iran and Telegram clusters.
6. Bounded test checklist: device, app version, network, protocol, server region, date/time, reconnect and failure behaviour.
7. FAQ matching PAA questions.
8. Sources and methodology with official protocol/provider documentation and a dated DataForSEO note.

## Conversion rules

- Use the shared editorial shortlist with `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and `/api/click` tracking.
- Keep prices and “visit” buttons clickable only where the provider offer is current and clearly labelled as affiliate.
- No pop-up, exit intent, invented speed percentages, permanent access claims, or “best for everyone” language.
- Add a natural inline link to the comparison page and relevant country/travel pages before the first commercial card.
