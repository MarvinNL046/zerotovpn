# Travel VPN editorial brief

**Canonical URL:** `/guides/vpn-for-travel`  
**Primary market:** United States / English  
**Intent:** informational with a bounded commercial comparison section

## Query signals

The current US/English dossier surfaces `vpn for travel`, `best vpn for travel`, `vpn for international travel`, `vpn for hotel wifi` and `vpn for traveling abroad`. The strongest retained non-zero historical volumes are 720, 480, 140, 110 and 70 respectively; DataForSEO returned no current volume for these rows, so the values are directional and are not treated as current demand.

PAA questions focus on whether a VPN is needed while travelling, hotel/airport Wi-Fi, international travel, free options, downside/risk and whether location switching lowers booking prices. The page answers these directly without promising access, savings or legality.

## Evidence boundary

- U.S. State Department communication-abroad guidance supports device updates, trusted VPN/VoIP options, Wi-Fi caution and backup communication planning.
- CISA travel guidance supports treating open public Wi-Fi as a risk for sensitive transactions.
- GOV.UK China travel advice is used as an example of destination-specific internet and legal context; it is not a universal travel rule.
- Provider pages establish current plan/features; they do not prove a connection on every hotel, airport, ISP or destination network.

## Page architecture

1. Intent-matched hero, last-reviewed date and disclosure.
2. Contextual provider shortlist with `sponsored nofollow` links.
3. What a travel VPN can and cannot do.
4. Before-departure preparation and recovery checklist.
5. Decision table for install/recovery, network fit, disconnect behaviour and commercial terms.
6. PAA FAQ with FAQ schema.
7. Official sources and links to restricted-network, obfuscation, protocol and Best VPN pages.

## Compliance gates

- No “essential as your passport”, “VPN required”, “works in China” or guaranteed-savings wording.
- No unauthorised coupon, urgency, popup or incentive.
- Affiliate links appear only inside an explicit travel-provider comparison context and use the shared disclosure/rel policy.
- Local laws and destination restrictions remain the reader’s responsibility; link dated official guidance.
